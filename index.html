Three modes

Three ways in.

Dream for the night. Daily for the day. Eureka for the idea that shows up at the wrong hour. One Nora across all three, with a memory you can see.

Mode 01 · Dream

Dream

Catch the night before it fades, even half-awake.

  • Speak it or type it in fragments. Plot holes are fine.
  • Nora remembers your past threads and asks the one question worth asking.
  • Vivid dreams can become dream cards with their own artwork, pinned to a growing album.

Mode 02 · Daily

Daily

Talk the day through, or just check in.

  • A rough meeting, a good stretch, something you can't put down: say it plainly.
  • Nora listens, builds on what you've told her before, and never grades you.
  • Mention Friday's big day once, and she'll ask you how it went.

Mode 03 · Eureka

Eureka

Catch the idea while it still has energy.

  • Drop in the loose thought before daylight edits it.
  • Nora keeps the charge and helps you shape it without sanding off the edge.
  • Pick a starting angle, or let it wait in your Eureka inbox until you're ready.

Alongside the three: Mindful wind-downs read aloud in Nora's voice, Insights with sleep context from Apple Health, read-only and never a score, and the Dreamcatcher album, where a vivid dream becomes a card with its own artwork.

Privacy, by design

Your life is your life.

Common Questions

Is this a replacement for therapy?
No, and it doesn't try to be. Somnora is not a replacement for therapy or medical care, but it can be a genuinely useful tool alongside it: the patterns Nora helps you notice (recurring themes, sleep context, what keeps returning) make rich, concrete context to bring into conversations with your therapist, or anyone helping you take care of yourself.
What does Nora remember, and can I change it?
Nora builds on what you've shared in Dream and Daily: recurring themes, dream symbols, life context, and personal details she inferred. Every item is listed under Nora Memory in Settings, where you can correct it, mark it no longer current, forget it, or clear all of it. Memory is optional.
How often does Nora reach out?
At most once a day, and only when she actually noticed something worth your attention: a follow-up on something you mentioned, a pattern that's forming, a rough stretch of sleep. If nothing has earned it, she stays quiet. Silence is a feature.
What does Apple Health add?
Sleep duration, stages, and timing context, so your reflections sit beside something measurable instead of floating on their own. It's always an explicit, read-only opt-in, and Nora describes trends in plain language, never scores.
Does Nora have a voice?
Yes. Morning capture is voice-first: you can talk before you're fully awake and Nora transcribes as you speak. She can also read wind-downs and meditations aloud in a warm, natural voice. And you can always just type.
