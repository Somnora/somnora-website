Somnora for iPhone and Apple Watch

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 28, 2026.

This policy covers Somnora for iPhone and Apple Watch, version 1.2 and later. It explains what stays on your device, what reaches Somnora's servers and why, and how to delete it.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your journal entries, conversations, Eureka ideas and documents, check-ins, drafts and most app settings are stored on your device.
  • Apple Health data is read only with your permission, and Somnora never writes to Apple Health.
  • When you speak instead of typing, Apple's speech recognition turns your voice into text. Apple may process that audio on its own servers under Apple's privacy policy.

What Somnora sends to its servers, and why

  • Messages to Nora. When you talk to Nora, your message, the recent conversation and the context Nora needs are sent to Somnora's server. That context can include your saved Nora memory and, if you allow Apple Health, a short sleep summary. Google's Gemini models on Google Cloud's Vertex AI write the reply. Under Google's terms, Google does not use this data to train its models. Google may cache requests for up to 24 hours to answer faster, and may log prompts to detect abuse of its services.
  • Bedside capture. If you record a dream by voice or add a sketch, the recording, the sketch and any notes are sent to Somnora's server so Gemini can write up the dream. Somnora's server does not keep the recording or the sketch.
  • Dreamcatcher art. When you create a Dreamcatcher image, your dream's text, mood and tags are sent to Somnora's server, and Google's Imagen model draws the picture. The finished image is stored in Somnora's cloud storage under your account ID so it can be delivered to your devices.
  • A photo of yourself, only if you add one. Somnora sends the photo once to turn it into a short written description of how you look, so Dreamcatcher can draw you. The photo is never stored, and only the words you approve are saved.
  • Nora's memory. Things you ask Nora to remember, and things Nora notes about you, are stored with your account so Nora can recall them. You can review, correct or clear them in Settings, under Nora Memory.
  • Safety signals. If a conversation suggests you may be in crisis, Somnora records that a safety alert happened. It keeps your account ID and the time, not the message.
  • Desktop Workbench, if you pair one. Conversations you choose to sync to a paired Workbench are stored with your account.
  • Purchases. Apple processes every payment, and Somnora never receives card details. To unlock Premium, Somnora's server checks your purchase with Apple. It stores the transaction identifiers, whether the purchase was a test or a real one, and when it was checked, under a one-way hash of your account ID.

Accounts

Creating an account is optional. Without one, Somnora uses an anonymous account on this device. You can connect an email address to keep Nora's memory across devices.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Somnora shows no ads, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell your data. In-app analytics events stay on your device.

Deleting your data

  • Delete account, in Settings under Account, permanently deletes your Somnora account and the server data described above: Nora's memory, journal data saved with your account, Workbench sync data, safety-alert records, purchase records and your Dreamcatcher images. Somnora then removes its data from your iPhone.
  • Deleted Dreamcatcher images stay in Google Cloud Storage's 7-day recovery period, then are removed permanently.
  • Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription, so cancel it in the Settings app under your Apple Account, then Subscriptions.
  • Delete data on this device, in Settings under Data & Privacy, removes Somnora's data from this iPhone only.
  • Clear Nora memory, in Settings under Nora Memory, clears the memory saved with your account without deleting the account.

Contact

Questions about privacy or your data can be sent to privacy@somnora.app.
