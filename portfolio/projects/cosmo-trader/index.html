The project

What it does

Cosmo Trader takes the language and visual density of a market terminal and crosses it with the irreverence of a horoscope app. A holding gets a zodiac sign from a founding date, charts can carry lunar and retrograde overlays, and a portfolio can receive a shareable cosmic read. The app repeatedly states the important part: astrology is not a trading signal.

Real quote and historical data stay separate from the entertainment layer. Typed services fetch, cache, and normalize market records. Deterministic astronomy and astrology services calculate phases, sign seasons, transits, and compatibility. View models then compose those sources into charts, cards, horoscopes, portfolio diagnostics, and widget snapshots.

Product principles

01

Real data, playful framing

Quotes and holdings are factual inputs; the cosmic interpretation is labeled entertainment.

02

Deep-history charts

Price history can be aligned with dated moon phases, retrograde windows, and company cycles.

03

Portfolio import without trading

Holdings can be parsed and tracked, but the product has no brokerage execution path.

04

A deliberate signal boundary

Language and compliance guards prevent astrology or model copy from posing as financial advice.

System architecture

How the system moves

Market facts and cosmic interpretation travel through separate services before a view model combines them. That makes it possible to audit a price, test an astronomical calculation, and govern the final language independently.

Market fact to cosmic card

  1. 01 · Inputs

    Quotes, history, and holdings

    Remote market APIs, portfolio files, manual shares, and cached records enter through typed adapters.

  2. 02 · Data services

    Normalize, cache, diagnose

    Quote clients and history caches establish coverage, freshness, and a stable stock model.

  3. 03 · Astro engines

    Phases, signs, and transits

    Deterministic services compute the entertainment context from dates and orbital calendars.

  4. 04 · Framing

    View models and language policy

    Market and astro context are combined under not-financial-advice and signal-framing rules.

  5. 05 · Experience

    Charts, portfolio, horoscope

    SwiftUI renders correlation timelines, discovery, readings, roasts, and diagnostics.

  6. 06 · Surfaces

    Widget, notifications, optional cloud

    Bounded summaries reach the home screen while profile and referral state can use the backend.

The architecture preserves a non-negotiable distinction: market values can be factual, but any astrological correlation remains an entertainment overlay and never becomes an execution signal.
